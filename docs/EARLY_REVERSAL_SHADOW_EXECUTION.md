# OPTION-001 Early-Reversal Shadow — isolated, same-cycle paper comparison

The shadow always runs on the same scheduled OPTION-001 `scan-once` and `manage-once` cycles. Strict EMA50+VWAP directional checks still protect OPTION-001. A verified EMA50-trend WAIT routes the ordinary scan to `manage-once` with `SHADOW_EARLY_ENTRY_SCAN=1`, so the shadow alone can evaluate fresh eligible paper entries during the unchanged OPTION-001 entry window (09:45–12:00 ET).

The shadow records both **signals** and independently evaluated 0DTE **credit/debit spreads** and delegates paper opening/exit to `LocalPaperSimulation`, pointing it at a separate `results/early_reversal_shadow_paper_ledger.json`. The main `results/option_001_paper_ledger.json` is never touched by shadow execution. The shadow uses original option strategy risk, event, probability, strike-location and liquidity rules, and only removes the *EMA50 direction veto* when forming its earlier VWAP+EMA9/EMA20+relative-volume signal. It does not place real broker orders.

For trend-WAIT cycles, real option quotes may be absent from the main direction-first snapshot. A separate read-only `results/early_reversal_shadow_research.json` needs independently observed same-day SPXW/XSP contracts, current quote timestamps, Greeks, verified SPY five-minute bars and original `market_context_json` reference pricing. Collect it during each scheduled cycle, with held-leg quotes refreshed during management. Missing data means no trade and no assumed P/L. The original `results/robinhood_option_research.json` must not be contaminated or overwritten by shadow-only contract data.

Output: `results/option_001_early_reversal_shadow_latest.json` has decision/structure rejections, independent paper events and performance; `results/option_001_early_reversal_shadow_observations.jsonl` is append-only; and `results/early_reversal_shadow_paper_ledger.json` is the durable paper-position state. W/L, realized P/L and closed-trade-sequence drawdown are only scored from observed simulated fills and verified closures. Intratrade drawdown is a separate, not-yet-implemented measurement. Expiration outcomes without official settlement remain unresolved.

Regression suite: `PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m pytest -q`. An archived 10:26 CT November 12 research snapshot produced independent early-reversal signals and WATCH spread evaluations for SPX/XSP, not paper fills. A full live-scheduled shadow lifecycle with fresh read-only quotes remains to be verified.
